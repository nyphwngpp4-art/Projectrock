# Content Verification Register

Every fact the demo publishes, where it comes from, and what still needs the
practice's confirmation before launch.

## How this works

- `src/data/practice.ts`, `src/data/doctors.ts`, and `src/data/services.ts` are
  the single source of truth. Each fact carries a status and a source.
- **On practice site**: stated on the practice's current website
  (abileneeyeinstitute.com, reviewed 2026-10-01). Fine for the demo; confirm
  before launch.
- **Confirmed by practice**: confirmed directly with the practice.
- **Pending, not shown**: from directories or not yet sourced. These never
  render on a page; they are listed here so they can be resolved.
- The tables between the register markers are generated by
  `npm run content:register`. `npm run build` fails if they drift from
  `src/data`, so this document always matches what the site shows.
- Personal details from the practice's bios (family, hobbies) are left out on
  purpose.

## The confirmation call (short list)

1. Roster: are all six doctors currently seeing patients? (Dr. Preston appears
   on "Meet Our Doctors" but not in the current site's doctor menu.)
2. Dr. Phelan's credentials, verbatim. The current site says "Fellow of the
   American Board of Ophthalmology"; the ABO certifies diplomates, so confirm
   the intended wording.
3. Dr. McAdams: board certification and Spanish appear only in directory
   listings. Publish either only if confirmed.
4. Friday hours: 8:00 to 1:00 and 1:30 to 5:00 (contact block), or Monday to
   Friday 8 to 5 (body text)? The current site says both.
5. What are (855) 463-5490 and (800) 692-2020, and should either appear?
6. The surgery center's name: "Abilene Eye Institute Cataract and LASIK
   Surgery Center" (building sign), "Cataract and Refractive Surgery Center"
   (current site), or AmSurg's "Abilene Cataract and Refractive Surgery
   Center"?
7. Is there a patient portal? Any plans for online scheduling?
8. Rights to the practice photography (team, building, headshots) and original
   files for the new site.
9. Permission on file for the patient comments quoted from the current
   reviews page.
10. Current patient forms (the posted PDFs date from 2012 to 2015) and one
    current Notice of Privacy Practices.
